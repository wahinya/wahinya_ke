🏡 My Story

I was born and raised in Dagoretti South. These are not just streets to me — they are home. I watched my mother walk long distances because roads were never fixed. I saw my friends drop out of school because bursaries never came.

I worked hard, studied engineering, and built a career. But every time I came home, nothing had changed. The same potholes. The same broken promises. The same faces asking for votes and disappearing.

That is why I am running. Not to join the system — but to fix it.

⚙️ Why an Engineer?

Engineering taught me one thing: problems have solutions. You do not complain about a broken bridge — you design a new one, source materials, manage the budget, and build it.

That is exactly how I will approach leadership. No excuses. No vague promises. Just a clear plan, proper use of public funds, and measurable results that every resident of Dagoretti South can see and feel.

I have delivered projects on time and on budget throughout my career. I will do the same for this constituency.

🎯 What I Stand For

I stand for a Dagoretti South where every young person has a path forward — whether through bursaries, skills training, or job creation. A constituency where businesses can thrive and families can afford healthcare.

I stand for roads that are built and maintained. For water that flows. For a health centre in every ward. For a constituency office that is open every working day — not just during campaigns.

🚫 On Corruption

Let me be direct: corruption is theft. Every shilling that disappears into someone's pocket is a child without a bursary, a road with potholes, a clinic without medicine.

I will publish every expenditure. I will hold public participation forums every quarter. I will welcome audits. And I will never, ever use public office for personal gain.

This is not a campaign promise — it is a personal value I have held my entire career.

My Five Commitments to You

01

Education & Bursaries

No child in Dagoretti South should drop out because of school fees. I will fight for maximum bursary allocation and transparent distribution to every ward.

02

Roads & Infrastructure

Using my engineering background, I will oversee every road project personally — ensuring quality construction and value for money.

03

Youth Employment

A dedicated youth enterprise fund, skills training partnerships with companies, and internship programmes for every ward.

04

Healthcare Access

Upgraded health facilities in all five wards, affordable medicine, and mental health support — because health is a right, not a luxury.

05

Open Governance

Quarterly public participation forums, published budgets, and a constituency office that is open and accessible to every resident every working day.
